Cast — Lead Role
Erso Supund
Erso is brilliant, but not in a way that looks clean. His thoughts move faster than he can speak them, and he often trips over his own words trying to keep up. He’s observant, restless, and constantly thinking — even when he shouldn’t be. There’s a playful, slightly chaotic energy to him. He can be annoying, competitive, even a little stubborn, but never in a way that feels cruel. He’s genuine. You believe him immediately.
He doesn’t command attention. He earns it slowly — then suddenly.
Special Abilities: Expresses layered intelligence through awkwardness, naturalistic stuttering and physical fidgeting, balances humor with sincerity, carries chaotic but grounded energy.
